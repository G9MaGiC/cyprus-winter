# Do No Significant Harm (DNSH) — PRE-SEED/0526

**Draft for the compatibility gate.** Not a lifecycle assessment. Do not invent carbon tonnes or green badges.

Cyprus Winter shifts visitor attention from July–August coastal peaks toward **November–March** rural and mountain days (villages, wine routes, Troodos trails). That is the environmental claim: **season spread**, not offset maths.

## Six EU DNSH objectives (proportionate)

| Objective | How this prototype avoids significant harm |
|-----------|---------------------------------------------|
| Climate mitigation | No new heavy infrastructure. Software + existing venues. Encourages off-peak travel rather than extra summer capacity. |
| Climate adaptation | Trail cards surface hiker **condition reports** (or “no report”). Plan copy tells visitors to skip unsafe or snowed Troodos routes. Does not invent Forestry Department feeds. |
| Water / marine | No water-intensive product. Does not promote unsustainable beach-day volume in winter as if it were summer. |
| Circular economy | Digital product; winery heroes use partner or documented CC assets, not disposable campaign print. |
| Pollution | No physical plant. Booking is request/email, not a new high-traffic event series. |
| Biodiversity | Directs people to **existing** trails and villages. Does not open unofficial paths. Copy prefers villages over inventing new wilderness access. |

## What the product actually says

The Plan sustainability strip (shipped) tells visitors to prefer villages, check Troodos conditions, and use **airport buses only where `src/data/airports` has a real source**. It does **not** display fabricated kg CO₂.

## What we will not claim in IRIS

- Quantified emissions savings without a named methodology and dataset
- “Carbon-neutral tourism”
- Blockchain or tokenised offsets
- That the app replaces Visit Cyprus safety or entry rules
